RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on June 1, 2026, by and between Crestview Realty Management LLC ("Landlord"), and Jordan Miller ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the real property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield, IL 62704 ("Premises"), for use solely as a private residential dwelling.

2. TERM AND AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
The initial term of this Lease shall commence on July 1, 2026, and expire on June 30, 2027. UNUSUAL RENEWAL CONDITION: Tenant must provide written notice of non-renewal exactly between sixty (60) and fifty-five (55) days prior to lease expiration (a strict 5-day opt-out window). If written notice is not received by Landlord within this specific 5-day window, this Agreement shall automatically renew for a successive 12-month period at a fifteen percent (15%) increase in Monthly Rent.

3. RENT PAYMENT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,400.00 ("Monthly Rent"), payable on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. Rent payments received after 11:59 PM on the third (3rd) day of the month shall incur an immediate late fee of $150.00 plus $25.00 per day thereafter until paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,800.00 (equivalent to two months' rent) as a Security Deposit. The Security Deposit shall be held to guarantee Tenant's full compliance with all covenants herein. Landlord shall return the deposit, less any allowable deductions, within sixty (60) days following complete vacancy.

5. EARLY TERMINATION AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
Tenant may not terminate this Agreement prior to expiration without written consent of Landlord. In the event Tenant vacates or abandons the Premises prior to June 30, 2027, Tenant shall immediately forfeit the entire Security Deposit and shall pay an early termination penalty equal to four (4) months' rent ($9,600.00), in addition to remaining liable for monthly rent until Landlord re-rents the unit.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean, sanitary, and good condition. Tenant is strictly responsible for all maintenance, repairs, and service calls costing under $350.00 per incident, including plumbing clogs, appliance servicing, HVAC filter replacements, and pest control. Landlord shall be responsible only for structural repairs to the foundation and roof, provided damage was not caused by Tenant neglect.

7. LANDLORD ACCESS AND INSPECTION
Landlord and its designated agents reserve the right to enter the Premises at any time between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM without prior written notice for general inspection, maintenance assessment, or showing the unit to prospective buyers or appraisers. In cases of suspected emergency, Landlord may enter at any hour without notice.

8. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
Tenant agrees to establish and pay directly for all electricity, natural gas, water, sewer, trash collection, and internet services servicing the Premises starting on the Commencement Date.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Lease shall be construed under the laws of the State of Illinois. Any dispute arising under or relating to this Agreement shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Cook County, Illinois. Tenant explicitly waives all rights to trial by jury and waives any right to participate in or initiate class-action litigation against Landlord.
