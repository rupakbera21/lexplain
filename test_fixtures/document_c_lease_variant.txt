RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (STANDARD VARIANT)

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on June 1, 2026, by and between Crestview Realty Management LLC ("Landlord"), and Jordan Miller ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the real property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, Springfield, IL 62704 ("Premises"), for use solely as a private residential dwelling.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
The initial term of this Lease shall commence on July 1, 2026, and expire on June 30, 2027. Either party may prevent renewal by providing at least thirty (30) days advance written notice prior to expiration. If no notice is given, tenancy continues on a month-to-month basis under identical terms.

3. RENT PAYMENT
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,750.00 ("Monthly Rent"), payable on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. A grace period is provided through the fifth (5th) day of each month, after which a flat late fee of $50.00 shall apply.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $2,750.00 (equivalent to one month's rent) as a Security Deposit. Landlord shall return the full deposit, less itemized deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear, within fourteen (14) days following complete vacancy.

5. EARLY TERMINATION
Tenant may terminate this Agreement prior to expiration upon giving sixty (60) days advance written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to one (1) month's rent ($2,750.00). Upon payment of this fee, Tenant is released from all further rent obligations.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the Premises in good repair and comply with all housing codes. Tenant shall only be responsible for minor repairs costing under $50.00 per incident, such as changing lightbulbs and smoke detector batteries. All appliance servicing, plumbing, and HVAC systems shall be promptly repaired at Landlord's sole expense.

7. LANDLORD ACCESS AND NOTICE
Landlord agrees not to enter the Premises without giving Tenant at least twenty-four (24) hours advance written notice, except in cases of bona fide emergency. Permitted visits shall occur only during reasonable business hours (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday through Friday).

8. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
Landlord shall provide water, sewer, and municipal trash collection services at no additional cost. Tenant shall pay directly for electricity, natural gas, and internet services.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. The parties retain all statutory tenant rights and access to local municipal housing court remedies.
